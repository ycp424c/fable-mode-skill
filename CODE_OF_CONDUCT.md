# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

我们希望这里是可以认真讨论、坦诚纠正、安心贡献的地方。本准则适用于项目的 Issues、Pull Requests 和其他由维护者管理的项目讨论空间。

## 相处方式

- 尊重不同经验、语言、背景和身份的参与者。
- 讨论具体观点、内容和证据；提出异议时说明理由，接受有依据的纠正。
- 不进行人身攻击、歧视、骚扰、威胁、性化言论或持续挑衅。
- 未经允许，不公开他人的私人信息、密钥或非公开对话。
- 尊重他人的时间和边界，不发送垃圾内容或反复施压。

## 反馈与处理

维护者为 [@ycp424c](https://github.com/ycp424c)。公开讨论中的问题可通过相关评论链接提醒维护者；需要私下处理时，可先[创建只请求私密联系渠道的 Issue](https://github.com/ycp424c/fable-mode-skill/issues/new)，不写事件细节或个人信息，待建立私密渠道后再提供材料。也可以使用 GitHub 的 **Report abuse** 功能向平台举报；平台举报由 GitHub 独立处理。

维护者会根据事实、严重程度和重复情况处理，措施可包括要求更正、提醒、隐藏或移除内容、限制参与或封禁。处理时应尊重报告者隐私，只在必要范围内分享信息。对处理有异议时，可通过同样渠道提供新的事实或说明。

## English

This code applies to Issues, Pull Requests, and other project spaces managed by the maintainer. Treat people of different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and languages with respect. Discuss specific ideas and evidence, accept reasoned corrections, and respect others' time and boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized comments, persistent provocation, spam, and sharing private information without permission are unacceptable.

The maintainer is [@ycp424c](https://github.com/ycp424c). Flag problems in public discussions with a link to the relevant comment. For sensitive incidents, first [open an issue requesting a private contact channel](https://github.com/ycp424c/fable-mode-skill/issues/new), without incident details or personal information. Share those details only after a private channel is agreed. GitHub's **Report abuse** feature is a separate reporting route handled by the platform.

Depending on the facts, severity, and repetition, responses may include a request for correction, a warning, hiding or removing content, participation restrictions, or a ban. Reports should be handled with respect for privacy and information shared only as needed. You may provide further facts or appeal through the same contact process.
